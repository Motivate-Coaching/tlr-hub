Building Decision Self-Trust

You have made good decisions before. You will again.

Self-trust is not confidence. It is the accumulated evidence that you can rely on yourself — even when things are uncertain. This tool helps you build that evidence deliberately, by looking at what your decision-making has already shown you and naming what you trust about yourself going forward.
