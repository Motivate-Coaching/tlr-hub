Momentum That Lasts

Not constant progress — an approach that keeps working through real life.

The goal is not to sustain perfect momentum. The goal is to build something robust enough to survive real life — the busy seasons, the setbacks, the days when motivation is nowhere to be found.

Reflection 1
What does lasting momentum look like for you — not in an ideal period, but in an ordinary one?
Reflection 2

Reflection 3
