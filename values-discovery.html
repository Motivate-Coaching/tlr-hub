Your choices already know what you value. Do you?

Most people sense a gap between the life they're living and the one they know they're capable of. They can't always explain it, but it shows up in the choices they make, the things that frustrate them, and the moments they feel most like themselves.

This 8-minute exercise reads that evidence and shows you exactly what's going on: what you're actually living by, where the gap is, and one practical step for each value that matters most to you.
