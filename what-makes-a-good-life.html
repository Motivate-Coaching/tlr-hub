The evidence: what actually contributes?

There's a lot of advice out there about what makes a good life. This week, we're looking at what the research consistently shows makes a real difference. Read through the key areas below and note down what lands for you and anything that surprises you.

Area What the research says My Notes
Meaning and purpose Having a sense of purpose is strongly linked to wellbeing, resilience and life satisfaction.
Close relationships Deep, supportive relationships are one of the most consistent predictors of a good life.
Physical health Movement, good sleep and looking after physical health significantly impact mood, energy and longevity.
Autonomy and choice Feeling a sense of control and freedom in how we live and work is closely linked to wellbeing.
Contribution Feeling that we make a positive difference to others increases happiness and life satisfaction.
Present-moment experience Being able to notice and enjoy everyday experiences is linked to greater wellbeing.
Growth and learning Continuing to learn and develop is associated with higher satisfaction and resilience.
