The Compound Effect

The small things you do every day matter far more than the big things you do occasionally.

You don't rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems. Momentum is not built in moments — it is built in the quiet, consistent actions no one else sees.

Day 7 looks the same as Day 1. Day 28 looks nothing like either.
The early days of a new habit look almost flat. But the curve is forming beneath the surface. Every consistent action bends the line — slowly, then dramatically. The compound effect does not care about effort. It cares about consistency.
