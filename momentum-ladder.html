The Momentum Ladder

From where you are to where you want to be — one rung at a time.

Momentum rarely comes from motivation. It usually comes from making the first step small enough to begin. This tool helps you break down what matters most into a single step so small that it feels almost impossible not to take.

How to use this tool
  1. Start at the top — write down what you most want to move toward.
  2. Work downward — break that goal into progressively smaller steps.
  3. Make the bottom step tiny — small enough you would take it even on a hard day.
  4. Score your likelihood, then commit to your step. Momentum begins there.
