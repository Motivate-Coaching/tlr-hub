The Momentum
Ladder

Momentum rarely comes from motivation.
It usually comes from making the first step small enough to begin.
Use this tool to break down what matters most into a single step small enough that it feels almost impossible not to take.
How to use this tool
1
Start at the top. Write down what matters most to you right now.
2
Break it down. Fill in the rungs — each one a smaller step toward the top.
3
Make the bottom step tiny. Small enough it feels almost impossible not to do.
4
Take that step. Momentum begins there.
