The Cost of the
Unmade Decision

Every decision you are not making is still costing you something.

Indecision is not a neutral state. It is a decision — to keep paying the cost of not deciding. This tool walks you through one decision you have been avoiding, step by step, so you can finally put it down or move it forward.

1
Name it
What is the decision you have been avoiding?
2
Count the cost
What is this indecision actually costing you? Think about energy, time, clarity, peace of mind, and opportunities.
3
Examine the fear
What are you afraid will happen if you decide?
4
Question the fear
Is that fear based on fact, or on story? What is the most likely outcome if you decide?
5
Choose your next move
You do not have to decide everything today. What is the smallest decision you can make right now that moves this forward?

My commitment

I commit to…
Deciding is not the risk. Continuing to not decide is. The moment you decide, the energy you were spending on indecision becomes available for living.
Light action this week
Take the smallest possible step you identified and do it before the week is out. You do not need to resolve everything — just move.
