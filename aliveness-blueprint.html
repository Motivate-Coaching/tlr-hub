Aliveness Blueprint

What makes you come alive? Map it. Choose more of it.

Aliveness is not about peak experiences. It is about knowing what lights you up and choosing more of it in your everyday life. This blueprint helps you get specific — category by category — so that "feeling more alive" becomes something you can actually act on, not just wish for.

For each category below, write what creates aliveness for you in that area. Trust your instincts — there are no wrong answers here.
