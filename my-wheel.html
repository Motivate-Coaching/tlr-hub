Your Balance Wheel

The Balance Wheel is a simple, powerful snapshot of where you are in life right now. You choose 8 areas that are priorities for you at this moment, then rate your level of satisfaction with each one. From there, you define what a 10 would feel like — and identify one small action to start moving.

It takes about 10 minutes. You can come back and do it again anytime — with different areas, at a different season of life. This is a snapshot, not a commitment.

  • Choose 8 areas that feel most relevant to your life right now
  • Rate your satisfaction in each area — honestly, and by your own measure
  • Define your 10/10 — what would it look and feel like for you?
  • Set one +1 action for each area — small, real, doable
