Your Balance Wheel

The Balance Wheel is a simple, powerful snapshot of where you are in life right now. You choose 8 areas, rate each one, define what 10/10 looks like — and then we help you find one small action to move forward in each.

It takes about 10 minutes to set up. Once it's done, everything in The Living Room becomes more personal.

  • Choose 8 areas of life that matter most to you right now
  • Rate each area from 1–10, honestly
  • Define your 10/10 — what would it look and feel like?
  • Set one +1 action for each area — small, real, doable
