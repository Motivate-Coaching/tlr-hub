Your privacy

  • What you write is yours. Your notes, plan and answers are only ever seen by you, unless you choose to share something. During the founding tester pilot, Stuart can see the name of your habit and how often you show up, never your notes.

The full details are below.

Who we are

Let’s Go! is made by Stuart Fairbairns, trading as Motivate Coaching, Cape Town, South Africa. Stuart is responsible for your information (the “controller” under UK and EU data protection law, and the “responsible party” under South Africa’s POPIA). Contact: stuart@motivate-coaching.com. A postal address is available on request.

What Let’s Go! is, and isn’t

Let’s Go! is a self-help tool for building habits, trying something new for a set number of days, and noticing what helps. It isn’t medical, psychological or professional advice, and it isn’t a substitute for it. If you have a health condition, or you’re thinking about a change that could affect your health, please talk to a qualified professional first. You decide what you take on and how, and you’re responsible for those choices.

Our lawful basis

  • To provide the app you asked for (your tracker, plan, sign-in and sync): performance of our agreement with you.
  • Morning nudges by email or phone notification: your consent. You switch them on, and you can switch them off or pause them in Settings.
  • Feedback, usage stats, and keeping the app secure and working: our legitimate interest in running and improving a useful service. Usage stats stay with us, are never sold or used for advertising, and you can switch them off at any time.

We will only send you marketing emails if you separately opt in, and you can unsubscribe any time.

The idea helper (“What I’m hearing”)

When you ask for challenge ideas, the answers you wrote are sent to Anthropic, our AI provider, so it can suggest areas to focus on and some challenges for you. Nothing you write is saved by the app on our side, and Anthropic’s terms don’t allow it to train its models on what we send. So the idea helper works before you add your email, the app quietly creates a guest account on your phone. It holds no name or email, only a random ID and how many ideas you’ve asked for today, so the daily limit works. When you add your email, the guest account is deleted.

Who helps us run the app

We use a small number of trusted providers who process data only on our instructions, under data processing terms:

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in, hosted in London, UK.
  • Resend: sends nudge emails (United States).
  • Anthropic: the idea helper described above (United States).
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla push services: deliver phone notifications if you switch them on.
  • GitHub Pages, Google Fonts and jsDelivr: host the app and its fonts and code, and see basic technical data such as your IP address when the app loads.

Where your information leaves the UK, the EU or South Africa, we rely on these providers’ data processing terms, including standard contractual clauses where required, so it stays protected.

How long we keep it

We keep your Let’s Go! data while you use the app. Usage stats are deleted after 12 months. If you don’t open it for 12 months, we delete what you’ve written in it. You can delete it yourself at any time in Settings. If you’ve opted in to emails, we keep your email address for that until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your information, object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent. Most of this you can do yourself in the app:

  • Download my data and Delete my Let’s Go! data are in Settings.
  • Deleting the app from your phone doesn’t delete what’s saved to your account, so use Delete in Settings, or email us.
  • For anything else, email stuart@motivate-coaching.com and we’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (UK), your local data protection authority (EU), or the Information Regulator (South Africa).

If you’re in the United States

Let’s Go! is a self-help habit app, not a healthcare provider or health plan, so the US health privacy law HIPAA doesn’t apply to it. Some US state laws, such as Washington’s My Health My Data Act, protect “consumer health data”, which can include wellness goals and habits. So, wherever you live:

  • We ask first. We only save your habit, progress and notes to an account after you tick the box agreeing to it. Before that, everything stays on your phone.
  • We never sell it, never share it for advertising, and never use it to make decisions about you.
  • You can see, download or delete it any time in Settings, and withdraw your agreement by deleting your data.
  • If something goes wrong, such as a data breach, we’ll tell you and any regulator that needs to know, as US rules including the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule require.

To use any of these rights, or to appeal a decision we’ve made about a request, email stuart@motivate-coaching.com.
