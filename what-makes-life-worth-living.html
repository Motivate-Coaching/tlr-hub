1

The ingredients of a life well lived

These are the factors research most consistently links to wellbeing and life satisfaction. For each one, consider: how present is this in your life right now, and what does it actually look like?

1Relationships

Quality of connection — not the number of people, but the depth and ease of the relationships you have.

2Health

Physical health and energy — how your body feels to live in and what that enables.

3Meaning

A sense that what you do matters — that your life has direction, purpose, or significance beyond the day-to-day.

4Agency

A feeling of authorship over your own life — that your choices reflect who you are, not just what's expected of you.

5Growth & Competence

Learning, developing, getting better at things that matter to you — feeling capable and progressing.

6Material Security

Enough — not necessarily wealth, but sufficient stability to not be preoccupied with survival.

7Contribution

Giving something back — making a difference to others, however large or small.

8Enjoyment

Pleasure, fun, rest, playfulness — moments where you're fully present because you want to be.
