Where Your Time Goes

Seeing the reality, not the intention.

Most of us have a sense of how we want to spend our time. This tool is not about that — it is about what actually happened. Looking honestly at one day, without editing it, is how you begin to understand where your time is really going.

A full day — where did yours actually go?

1
What actually filled my time today?
No ideal version — just what actually happened. List the things that took your time today.
2
Where was I choosing?
Which parts of today were conscious decisions — things you intentionally chose to give your time to?
3
Where did I react?
What pulled you in without you really deciding to go? Where were you responding to others' urgency, habit, or expectation rather than your own priorities?
4
What does this tell me?
One simple, honest observation. What does today reveal about how your time is actually moving through your life right now?
You cannot change what you have not seen clearly. Awareness is the first step to getting your time back.
Light action this week
Run this reflection at the end of one day this week. Just 5 minutes. Notice what the day actually contained.
