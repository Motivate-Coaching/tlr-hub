Quick setup — takes 2 minutes:
1. Sign up free at unsplash.com/developers
2. Create a new application (call it anything — "TLR Journal")
3. Copy your Access Key
4. Open my-journal.html in a text editor, find const UNSPLASH_ACCESS_KEY = '' near the top of the <script> and paste your key between the quotes
5. Save and push — image search will be live!
