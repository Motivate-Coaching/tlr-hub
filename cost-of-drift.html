The Cost of Drift

Drift is rarely dramatic. It is a slow accumulation of small moments of not choosing.

Theme: Autopilot and Drift · The Cost of Drift
Drift does not announce itself. It arrives quietly, one unchecked day at a time.

Question 1 of 3
