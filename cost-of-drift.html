The Cost of Drift

Seeing what staying on autopilot is really costing you.

Drift is what happens when life keeps moving but you stop steering. It rarely feels dramatic — it just quietly adds up. These three questions help you see what staying on autopilot is actually costing you, so you have a real reason to choose something different.

1
How long has this felt off?
Think about an area of your life that has felt misaligned, flat, or disconnected. How long has it been like this? Be honest — even if the answer surprises you.
2
What is this costing me over time?
Think broadly — energy, relationships, opportunities, confidence, joy. What has drifting in this area cost you, quietly, over time? Write it all down.
3
What might happen if nothing changes?
If this drift continues — another year, another five years — what becomes true? This is not about fear. It is about using an honest picture of the future as a reason to move.
Light action this week
Pick one area where you have been drifting and, just for today, make one small intentional choice in that space. Not a plan. Just one choice.
