About this tool

Autopilot is what happens when life runs on repeat — without you really choosing it. This tool helps you surface the patterns you've been running on so you can actually see them.

You can't change what you haven't noticed. This is the noticing step.

How to approach it
  • This isn't about judgment. Just honest observation — as if you're watching yourself from the outside.
  • Take your time on column 1 before moving across. Let 3–5 things come to mind naturally.
  • Column 3 is often where the insight lands. Don't rush it.
  • Section 4 becomes your one focus for the week ahead. Pick the one that feels most alive right now.
