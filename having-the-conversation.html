Having the Conversation

Respect yourself. Respect them. Respect what needs to be said.
Use this compass to guide you in the moment.
1
Me
What do I need to say honestly?
What matters enough that I shouldn't minimise, avoid or compromise it simply to make the conversation easier?
2
Them
What do I need to understand?
What might I not know yet? What questions could help me understand their experience rather than assume it?
Stay clear on what matters.
Stay curious about what you don't know.
4
The Conversation
What needs to stay in the room?
Be honest
Stay curious
Keep listening
3
Us
What do we both care about here?
What shared outcome, relationship, responsibility or intention can we come back to when things get hard?

Keep in mind

In the Moment
If things get tricky, come back here.
Pause
Take a breath. Stay calm.
Listen
Focus on understanding, not responding.
Return to Purpose
What are we trying to achieve here?
