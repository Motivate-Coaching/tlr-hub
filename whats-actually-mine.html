What's Actually Mine

Clarifying what belongs to you, and what does not.

Not everything asking for your attention deserves your ownership. This tool helps you separate what is genuinely yours to carry from what you have picked up along the way — so you can hold your real responsibilities well and let go of the rest.

Examples to get you started
Needs my attentionMy health, my work, my relationships, my finances
I've taken onOther people's urgency, managing everyone's feelings, expectations I haven't questioned
Within my controlHow I respond, what I prioritise, where I give my attention
