The Gap

Noticing the difference between what you wanted to say and what you actually said.

There are moments in most days when what we want to say and what we actually say are different. The gap between them is where pressure, habit, and the need to please quietly shape our responses. Seeing the gap is where the work begins.

To get you started Think of recent moments where you said yes when you wanted to say no, softened something you wanted to say clearly, or agreed when you disagreed. Use the columns below to capture those moments. Two examples have been filled in to show you how.
