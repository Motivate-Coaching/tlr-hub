Your privacy

Last updated 25 October 2026

  • What you write is yours. Your notes, plan and answers are only ever seen by you, unless you choose to share something. During the founding tester pilot, Stuart can see the name of your habit and how often you show up, never your notes.
  • You don’t need an account. Without one, everything stays on your phone. If you sign in, we save it securely so it’s backed up and your nudges can reach you.
  • We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never use it to make judgements about your health.
  • You’re in control. Download or delete your Show Up data any time in Settings.
  • Questions? Email stuart@motivate-coaching.com. A real person, Stuart, reads every one.

The full details are below.

Who we are

Show Up is made by Stuart Fairbairns, trading as Motivate Coaching, Cape Town, South Africa. Stuart is responsible for your information (the “controller” under UK and EU data protection law, and the “responsible party” under South Africa’s POPIA). Contact: stuart@motivate-coaching.com. A postal address is available on request.

What Show Up is, and isn’t

Show Up is a self-help tool for building habits, trying something new for a set number of days, and noticing what helps. It isn’t medical, psychological or professional advice, and it isn’t a substitute for it. If you have a health condition, or you’re thinking about a change that could affect your health, please talk to a qualified professional first. You decide what you take on and how, and you’re responsible for those choices.

What we collect and why

WhatWhy

We don’t ask for health information, and we don’t use anything you write to draw conclusions about your health. If you choose to mention something personal in your own words, it’s stored only so the app can show it back to you.

During the founding tester pilot, Stuart can see your email, the name of your habit and how often you show up, so he can support the pilot. He never sees your notes or anything else you write.

We use no advertising or tracking cookies. The app saves your progress in your browser’s own storage on your device, which it needs to work.

Our lawful basis

  • To provide the app you asked for (your tracker, plan, sign-in and sync): performance of our agreement with you.
  • Morning nudges by email or phone notification: your consent. You switch them on, and you can switch them off or pause them in Settings.
  • Feedback and keeping the app secure and working: our legitimate interest in running and improving a useful service.

We will only send you marketing emails if you separately opt in, and you can unsubscribe any time.

The idea helper (“What I’m hearing”)

When you tap “Reflect it back to me”, the answers you wrote in Help me choose are sent to Anthropic, our AI provider, so it can write a short reflection and some ideas for you. Nothing is saved by the app on our side, and Anthropic’s terms don’t allow it to train its models on what we send. It’s only available when you’re signed in, and only when you tap the button.

Who helps us run the app

We use a small number of trusted providers who process data only on our instructions, under data processing terms:

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in, hosted in London, UK.
  • Resend: sends nudge emails (United States).
  • Anthropic: the idea helper described above (United States).
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla push services: deliver phone notifications if you switch them on.
  • GitHub Pages, Google Fonts and jsDelivr: host the app and its fonts and code, and see basic technical data such as your IP address when the app loads.

Where your information leaves the UK, the EU or South Africa, we rely on these providers’ data processing terms, including standard contractual clauses where required, so it stays protected.

How long we keep it

We keep your Show Up data while you use the app. If you don’t open it for 12 months, we delete what you’ve written in it. You can delete it yourself at any time in Settings. If you’ve opted in to emails, we keep your email address for that until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download or delete your information, object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent. Most of this you can do yourself in the app:

  • Download my data and Delete my Show Up data are in Settings.
  • Deleting the app from your phone doesn’t delete what’s saved to your account, so use Delete in Settings, or email us.
  • For anything else, email stuart@motivate-coaching.com and we’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (UK), your local data protection authority (EU), or the Information Regulator (South Africa).

Age

Show Up is for adults aged 18 and over.

Keeping it safe

Your data is sent over encrypted connections, and each person can only ever access their own information. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you share, and we’ll tell you if something goes wrong that affects you.

Changes

If we change how we use your information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
