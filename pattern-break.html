The Pattern Break

Interrupting autopilot with one small, conscious shift.

You do not need a complete overhaul to break a pattern. You just need to interrupt it — once, then again, then again. This tool walks you through the four moves of a pattern break, so you can start right now with whatever is most on repeat.

Small changes interrupt big patterns.
1
Notice the pattern
What is one habit, reaction, or behaviour that has been on repeat? Describe it as specifically as you can — what it looks like, when it tends to happen, how it starts.
2
Catch it in the moment
What is the trigger — the moment just before the pattern kicks in? Catching a pattern early is how you get a window to choose differently. Describe the signal you will watch for.
3
Choose one small shift
What is one small, concrete thing you could do differently in that moment? It does not need to be a big change. Just different enough to interrupt the automatic response.
4
See what happens
After you try your shift, what did you notice? What felt different — even slightly? You do not need a big result. You just need enough data to go around the loop again.
Come back and fill this in after you've tried your shift this week.
Light action this week
Try your small shift just once this week. You are not changing a habit. You are just creating a gap in the pattern — one moment of choosing differently.
