The Stories I Inherited

Who taught me what life was supposed to look like?

Long before we were consciously deciding what mattered, we were learning what success, love, money, work, family, relationships, and a "good life" were supposed to look like. We absorbed these stories. This week, let's bring them into the light.

This Week's Experiment
Notice the word "should" — and ask one question when it appears.
Inherited stories often show up as "should" statements. This week, when you catch one — in your own thinking or in conversation — pause and ask:
I should be further ahead Parents should… I should earn more At my age I should… A successful person should…
When you catch a "should", ask:

Remember
Inheriting a story doesn't mean you have to keep living by it without ever reading it for yourself. Keep what serves you. Redefine what doesn't. You get to choose your next chapter.
